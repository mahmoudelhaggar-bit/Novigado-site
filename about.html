About Novigado

Who we are

Saudi Arabia pays twice for every tonne it throws away. We're here to end that.

Most of the Kingdom's recyclable materials still end up in landfill: resources the country has already paid for once, and pays for again as imports. Novigado exists to stop that loss. We recover plastic waste and manufacture finished products that go straight back into the Saudi economy.

We turn national targets into industrial capacity on the ground. Our Riyadh plant is the first step. Further waste streams, energy recovery, environmental advisory and community programmes follow, all under one name and one standard.

Our vision

A Kingdom where nothing is wasted, where every resource returns as value for people, industry and nature.

Our mission

To build Saudi Arabia's circular economy from Riyadh outward, turning plastic and other waste streams into certified, high-quality raw materials and finished end products through modern local manufacturing, Saudi talent, and partnerships with government, industry and communities, in support of Saudi Vision 2030 and the Saudi Green Initiative.

Novigado means renewal, from the Esperanto novigi, "to make new". Our mark joins three facets into one rising triangle, inspired by the Najdi architecture of Riyadh: collect, renew, return.
