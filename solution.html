Our solution

From unrecyclable plastic to durable infrastructure

Our solution

Composite manhole covers made from unrecyclable plastic, produced in Riyadh

We combine contaminated plastic that recyclers reject with mineral filler and steel reinforcement to make covers that last longer, weigh less and never rust.

Corrosion-free

Does not rust or crack in acidic sewer environments, for a longer service life.

Lightweight

A fraction of the weight of iron: easier to install and safer to lift.

Made from waste

Uses plastic rejects without washing, plus industrial foundry sand.

Made in Saudi Arabia

Local stock, short lead times and eligibility for local-content preference.

Built to EN 124

Designed for classes A15 and B125: footways, verges and car parks.

No scrap value

Low theft risk and lower replacement cost over its life.
