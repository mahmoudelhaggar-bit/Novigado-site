Who we are

Saudi Arabia pays twice for every tonne it throws away. We're here to end that.

Most of the Kingdom's recyclable materials still end up in landfill: resources the country has already paid for once, and pays for again as imports. Novigado exists to stop that loss. We recover plastic waste and manufacture finished products that go straight back into the Saudi economy.

We turn national targets into industrial capacity on the ground. Our Riyadh plant is the first step. Further waste streams, energy recovery, environmental advisory and community programmes follow, all under one name and one standard.

Our vision

A Kingdom where nothing is wasted, where every resource returns as value for people, industry and nature.

Our mission

To build Saudi Arabia's circular economy from Riyadh outward, turning plastic and other waste streams into certified, high-quality raw materials and finished end products through modern local manufacturing, Saudi talent, and partnerships with government, industry and communities, in support of Saudi Vision 2030 and the Saudi Green Initiative.

Our solution

Composite manhole covers made from unrecyclable plastic, produced in Riyadh

We combine contaminated plastic that recyclers reject with mineral filler and steel reinforcement to make covers that last longer, weigh less and never rust.

Corrosion-free

Does not rust or crack in acidic sewer environments, for a longer service life.

Lightweight

A fraction of the weight of iron: easier to install and safer to lift.

Made from waste

Uses plastic rejects without washing, plus industrial foundry sand.

Made in Saudi Arabia

Local stock, short lead times and eligibility for local-content preference.

Built to EN 124

Designed for classes A15 and B125: footways, verges and car parks.

No scrap value

Low theft risk and lower replacement cost over its life.

Proven by research

Performance validated through applied research at the American University in Cairo

Strategic partner · Sustainable development · Egypt

EcoStrategies for Sustainable Development is the first sustainable development company approved by the Egyptian Government (Investment Authority). It works on sustainable buildings, sustainable communities, waste management and Net Zero, and researches new recycling technologies with The American University in Cairo.

  • 75–100% recycling rates achieved in Egypt, through recycling research with AUC
  • Co-founding member of the Egypt Green Building Council; co-released Tarsheed, Egypt's green building rating system, and Tarsheed Communities, the first community rating system built on Net Zero pollution
  • LEED projects include Credit Agricole (Platinum), Dar Al Handasah and The Mall of Egypt (Gold), Zewail City and the Magdi Yacoub Global Heart Center
  • Sustainable communities for Tatweer Misr (Monte Galala) and Al Ahly Real Estate (Odysia); Tarsheed projects for Banque Misr and Egypt Post
  • Also delivers Net Zero design, eco-industrial parks, environmental impact assessment, cleaner production and EDGE consulting
ecostrategies.co
Technology partner · Sustainable materials · Egypt

Agrona, based in Cairo and Cologne, turns agricultural residues into high-performance wood panels using 100% bio-based resin and patent-pending composite technology, so no trees are cut and farmers gain a new source of income.

  • 1st place, JEC World Composites, Paris 2023: "the best sustainable composite technology"
  • 1st place, MIT Startup Competition (2021); People's Choice Award, Global Social Venture Competition, UC Berkeley (2019); SXSW finalist (2020, 2021)
  • EOS-certified panels that surpass European particleboard classes P1 to P5, with no detectable formaldehyde
  • Every tonne of farm residue becomes 1 m³ of panels, saves about 3 trees and furnishes 20 school units; up to 30–50% cheaper than imported panels
  • Field projects with LG and WABAG; alumnus of the Circular Valley Accelerator, Germany (2024)
